The rules of the game: The game is played by 2 players, and at the beginning of the game, each player must choose one character from the 5 available (players must choose different characters). The game has 2 modes: day and night. During the night, the characters' abilities change: Knight +10%, Wizard +20%, Thief +30%, Archer +40%, Vampire +50%. Each character has 2 attack abilities: attack and pet attack, which differ from one another to maintain balance: Thief beats Wizard and Archer, Knight beats Thief and Archer, Archer beats Wizard and Vampire, Wizard beats Vampire and Knight, Vampire beats Thief and Knight (in most cases). There is also a life regeneration ability in the order: Archer > Thief > Wizard > Knight > Vampire. The game ends when the life of one of the players reaches 0.
